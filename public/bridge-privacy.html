Unitify Bridge — Privacy

Updated 19 October 2026 · Independent, invite-only project. Not affiliated with Astana IT University.

Purpose and access

Unitify Bridge synchronizes your own Moodle academic records with your paired private Unitify Telegram account. Installing the extension does not grant access to the private bot. Synchronization requires an authorized Telegram account, explicit one-time-code pairing, and a signed-in Moodle session in your browser.

Data processed

The extension reads your Moodle user ID, enrolled course names and IDs, assignment names, descriptions, deadlines, submission and grading status, grade values, ranges, weights, percentages and teacher feedback, and attendance sessions, marks, points and remarks. The service associates these records with your Telegram chat ID and linked timetable/group. It also creates private grade-card images on your device. It does not upload your password, cookies, session keys, raw HTML or submitted homework files.

Storage and delivery

A local copy, cached activity links, a device pairing credential, settings and synchronization timestamps are stored in Chrome extension storage. Normalized records, account associations and grade images are sent over HTTPS to s.zhengisbay.com and stored using Cloudflare Workers and D1. The backend uses Telegram Bot API to deliver requested academic records, images and notifications to the paired account. Telegram and Cloudflare process data under their own policies. No advertising, analytics SDK or sale of academic data is used.

Timing and controls

Academic reads occur only after pairing and while a signed-in Moodle tab is available. The normal interval is 30 minutes, with daytime-only grade and attendance checks and temporary backoff when Moodle refuses requests. The pause switch stops collection and uploads; already-started requests may finish. Disconnecting Telegram revokes the device credential but does not delete previously saved server records or Telegram messages. Clearing local data removes the extension's local academic copy, not the server copy. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage.

Retention and deletion

Current academic snapshots and grade images remain on the server until removed by the project operator; they do not currently have a fixed automatic expiry. The synchronization-change outbox is limited to 500 records per account and records older than 90 days are removed during successful synchronization. This is not a promise that all service data expires after 90 days. To request deletion of your server-side academic data and account association, contact the operator at @nxxbruh on Telegram. Messages already delivered to Telegram are also subject to Telegram's storage and deletion behavior.

Read-only behavior

The extension does not submit assignments, take exams, edit grades or mark attendance. It uses the browser's existing session rather than signing in on another device. Normal page reads may appear in Moodle's access logs. Cached records reflect the last successful read, not a guaranteed live state.

Updates and diagnostics

Only timetable data, bounded numeric scheduling settings and version information are fetched as configuration. Executable code is packaged with the extension, not downloaded and executed from the server. The optional diagnostics export contains version, timestamps, counts and sanitized errors, without cookies, access credentials, grade values or course names. Infrastructure providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses under their policies.
